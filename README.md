# ASHFALL: RECLAMATION

Post-apocalyptic first-person survival shooter with base building, settlement management, trading, military and diplomacy. Built in **Godot 4.7** (GDScript, Forward+).

## Current state: Increment 19.6 (settlers build)
- **Increment 1:** first-person character (walk, sprint, crouch, jump) at **Outpost 17**: walled military outpost with a fortified gate, guard towers, enterable buildings, a warehouse, a motor pool and a crouch-only culvert.
- **Increment 2:** service rifle (full-auto, reload, recoil, spread, muzzle flash, bullet holes), reusable `HealthComponent`, and a **firing range** east of the gate.
- **Increment 3:** player health, fall damage, burning barrels, a 6 m range tower, damage feedback, death screen, and respawn via `GameMode`.
- **Increment 4:** raider enemies that see and hear you, path through the gate and around cover on a baked navmesh, and attack with a dodgeable machete swing.
- **Increment 5:** interaction (**E**, tap or hold), 6 item types and 22 pickups (scrap, cloth, fuel, electronics, ammo, medkits), raider loot drops, and **sound** for nearly everything (synthesized placeholders).
- **Increment 6:** **inventory** (Tab / I): 24 slots, stacking, 30 kg carry weight, item icons, drag to move or drop. Medkits are stored and used with **H** (2 s quick-heal). Rifle ammo is an inventory item.
- **Increment 6.5:** **aim down sights** (right-click or Q to toggle: red-dot sight, 1.4× zoom, tighter spread, less recoil) and **ladders**: all four guard towers can be climbed.
- **Increment 7:** **salvage containers**: 11 crates, footlockers, weapons lockers, a tool chest and wasteland stashes. Hold E to search (loot is rolled once), then move items with a two-sided transfer screen (double-click, drag, Take All with R).
- **Increment 8:** **save and load**: F5 quick save, F9 quick load (fn+F5 / fn+F9 on Mac). Saves the player, inventory, every container and the loot on the ground as versioned JSON. Also quieter footsteps and clanging lockers.
- **Increment 9 (Milestone 2):** **weapon slots** (1–4, mouse wheel): service rifle, sidearm pistol, pump shotgun (pellets, shell-by-shell reload; command post) and a marksman rifle with a 4× scope (up the north-west tower). Each gun has its own ammo.
- **Increment 10:** **crafting**: K to craft by hand anywhere; a workbench (warehouse) and weapons bench (command post); 13 recipes for components, ammo, bandages and medkits; break down pallets and furniture for wood.
- **Increment 11:** **gun crafting and attachments**: you now start with only a **machete** (slot 5) and craft a **pipe pistol** by hand. Gun parts (barrel, receiver, stock, trigger group, magazine) are made at the weapons bench and assembled into the pistol, shotgun, service rifle and marksman rifle. **Attachments** (red dot, 4× scope, suppressor, extended magazine, foregrip) are fitted on the bench's **Modify Weapons** tab and show on the gun. Every gun has iron sights.
- **Increment 12:** **a second region, Halden's Crossing**: follow the road south out of Outpost 17 and hold E at the road sign. A ruined town with enterable shops, a two-storey hotel and a burned church, rooftop and water-tower sightlines, and a walled raider camp held by tougher **Raider Veterans**. 21 containers, scrap heaps and appliance piles for crafting materials in bulk. You carry your gear between regions, each region remembers what you looted, and saves include every region.
- **Increment 13:** **construction**: craft structure kits (K → Structures), press **B** for build mode and place them with a green/red ghost: sandbag and wooden barricades, wooden and metal walls, a gate you open with E, spike traps, storage crates and a field workbench. Rotate with R, choose with the mouse wheel, move what you've built with F, repair with E and dismantle with X (within 30 s of placing, you get the kit back). Raiders path around what you build and **hack through** walls when you've shut them out. Everything you build is saved, per region.
- **Increment 14:** **fort building**: walls, fences and barricades **snap end to end** at any angle (R sets the joint: 45°, 90°…); wall kits place as **solid, window or doorway** (V); **platforms** you can stack and build on, reached by **stairs** (raiders follow) or **ladders** (they can't); a craftable **chain-link fence**; the gate opens with E anywhere on it. The outpost's own **props and loot containers can be moved (F) or scrapped (X)** for materials (trucks scrap only; containers once emptied). **Spike traps slow raiders to 1/4 speed.** Magic Mouse / trackpad swipes choose kits and weapons. Up to 1,000 structures per region, with merged meshes for speed.
- **Increment 15:** **the early zone**: Outpost 17 and Halden's Crossing are now one walkable zone laid out from the Regional Buildout Map. The outpost is on the west coast, Halden's ~530 m north-east up a new main road, with five more places marked out: Saint Mercy Clinic, Ash Creek Farms, Smuggler's Gulch, Black Ridge Mine and Northwatch Outpost. Roads, a railway and signposts join them, and a **compass** shows where everything is. The old border berms are gone: the zone's edge is an **ash storm** (it warns, then hurts, then stops you) and the coast. Raiders only appear near you. Old saves move into the zone automatically.
- **Increment 16:** **the five places, built out**: Saint Mercy Clinic (a two-storey hospital with wards, a pharmacy and a rooftop), Ash Creek Farms (barn and hayloft, farmhouse, a water tower to climb), Smuggler's Gulch (a den in a ring of red rock, held by Raider Veterans), Black Ridge Mine (headframe, mine office with a safe, a tunnel into the ridge) and Northwatch Outpost (a walled military post with an armory and guard towers). 21 buildings to go into, 45 containers with 9 new loot tables, 42 salvage piles, 31 pickups, 8 ladders, 3 crafting stations, raiders at every place, and lamp-lit interiors. See [`docs/increments/16_places.md`](docs/increments/16_places.md).
- **Increment 16.5:** **performance pass**: buildings merge into a few meshes per place when the zone loads; interiors are drawn only within 70 m; the sun's shadows use two cascades; buildings hide what's behind them (occlusion culling). About 60% fewer draw calls for the same picture. F3 now shows fps and draw calls, and `tools/benchmark.gd` measures a fixed route. See [`docs/increments/16_5_performance.md`](docs/increments/16_5_performance.md).
- **Increment 16.6:** **dying drops your gear**: everything you carry (backpack and guns, with their attachments and loaded rounds) goes into a **Your Gear** chest where you fell, marked on the compass with a beam of light over it; you respawn with only your machete. **Guns to find**: the Smuggler's Gulch gun cabinet and Northwatch's weapons lockers always hold a gun, and both places have better loot all round. A found gun goes straight into its slot. **A weapons bench in Northwatch's armory.** The Saint Mercy signpost now stands at the trail head instead of inside the staff house. See [`docs/increments/16_6_gear_and_guns.md`](docs/increments/16_6_gear_and_guns.md).
- **Increment 16.7:** **a bigger interface** that scales with the window (Retina, full screen), 25% bigger by default; **Ctrl/Cmd + = / − / 0** to change it. A bigger, clearer **compass**. The **inventory screen shows your five weapon slots**: click to take a gun out, right-click or drag to put it away. **Spare guns**: a second (or worse) gun goes into your backpack instead of turning into ammo, a better one swaps the old into the backpack; right-click, Equip or drag a spare onto its slot to swap it in. Spares weigh 1–4.6 kg and are carried unloaded, with attachments off. See [`docs/increments/16_7_inventory_and_spares.md`](docs/increments/16_7_inventory_and_spares.md).
- **Increment 16.8:** **backpacks**: a backpack slot in the inventory; craft a **Rucksack** (40 kg, 30 slots) by hand, find a **Military Pack** (50 kg, 36 slots: always in Northwatch's Quartermaster's Crate), craft a **Frame Pack** (60 kg, 42 slots) at a workbench, though it rattles and raiders hear you. Your pack drops with your gear when you die. **Double-tap W to sprint** (Minecraft-style), with a slight view widening; sprint time is counted for hunger later. See [`docs/increments/16_8_backpacks_and_sprint.md`](docs/increments/16_8_backpacks_and_sprint.md).
- **Increment 17:** **the map** (**M**): the whole zone seen from above, **filling in as you explore** (you see further from rooftops and the water tower). It marks the seven places (greyed until you've been), the crafting benches you've found, what you've built, **your dropped gear** and the ash-storm edge. **Right-click to drop a pin** (up to 10): pins show on the **compass** too. Drag, scroll or pinch to move and zoom; the side panel lists the places and pins with their distances (click one to look at it). Saved with your game. See [`docs/increments/17_map.md`](docs/increments/17_map.md).
- **Increment 18:** **a truck you can drive**, parked in Outpost 17's yard. **E** at the driver's door to get in, **W/S** drive and reverse, **A/D** steer, **Space** handbrake, **T** for a camera behind, **E** to get out. Up to 70 km/h. The **bed holds 48 slots / 400 kg** (E at the tailgate). It runs on **fuel**: 40 L tank, about 6.5 km a tank, and each Fuel Can adds 10 L at the fuel cap. Raiders hear the engine, and running into them hurts them. It's on the compass and the map, and saved where you leave it. See [`docs/increments/18_truck.md`](docs/increments/18_truck.md).
- **Increment 19:** **Ash Creek Farms becomes your first settlement.**
  - **Claiming it:** clear its raiders, then hold **E** at the notice board by the farmhouse porch. It turns green on the compass and map, and its raiders stop coming.
  - **Water and food:** fix the **pump** (3 Scrap Metal, 2 Metal Pipe, a Spring). **Farmers** turn water into **Fresh Vegetables** in the Farm Store (in the barn), and everyone eats from it. Go hungry too long and settlers stop working, then leave.
  - **Settlers:** **newcomers** walk in when there's a free bed (4) and food. **Survivors** at Saint Mercy, Halden's and Black Ridge come if you ask them (E).
  - **Guards:** they patrol and shoot raiders. Arm them with your spare guns, or stock the **Armory Locker**.
  - **Running it:** press **L** for the settlement screen: jobs, guns, stocks.

  See [`docs/increments/19_ash_creek_settlement.md`](docs/increments/19_ash_creek_settlement.md).
- **Increment 19.5:** **Ash Creek Farms laid out again.** The railway no longer runs through the farm: the farm sits beside the tracks, facing them, with a **level crossing** where its lane crosses (and at the main road near Halden's). The lane comes in under a gateway arch into a **yard**: the farmhouse at the back, the barn and silo on one side, the tool shed, pump house and water tower on the other, four **fields** in a row behind. **Food shelves** along the barn's back wall hold **1,200 vegetables** (was 240), with crates of produce showing as it fills; the settlement screen shows food against capacity. Old saves carry over. See [`docs/increments/19_5_farm_layout.md`](docs/increments/19_5_farm_layout.md).
- **Increment 19.6:** **settlers build.** Claiming Ash Creek lays out its plan as outlines: a **fence round the yard**, a **fence round the fields** and a **guard post at each corner**. Hand over materials at an outline (E), or leave them in the Farm Store, and settlers with the new **Builder** job put it up (fences a 4 m length at a time); hold E to help. In **build mode (B)** inside the farm you can plan a **Bunkhouse** (four more beds), **Storehouse** (1,200 more food), **Guard Tower**, **Guard Post** or **Farm Plot** (two more farmers). Guards take a post each; the settlement screen has a Building column. See [`docs/increments/19_6_settlers_build.md`](docs/increments/19_6_settlers_build.md).

## Run it
1. Install Godot 4.7 (standard, not .NET).
2. Godot Project Manager → **Import** → choose this folder's `project.godot`.
3. Press **F5**.

Controls: WASD move · Mouse look · Left click fire · Right click / Q aim (toggle) · 1–5 / mouse wheel switch weapons (5 = machete) · R reload · E interact · Tab/I inventory · M map (right-click: pin) · L settlement · H use medkit · K craft · F5 quick save · F9 quick load · Hold E on a road sign to travel · B build mode (click place, wheel/[ ] choose, V variant, R rotate, F move, E repair, X dismantle/scrap) · Shift or double-tap W sprint · E at the truck's door to drive (W/S, A/D, Space handbrake, T camera, E get out) · Space jump · C/Ctrl crouch · Esc free mouse · F3 debug (fps, draw calls) · Ctrl/Cmd + = / − / 0 interface size.

## After pulling an update — important
Open the project with **Edit** (not **Run**) in Godot's Project Manager, then press F5 or the ▶ button in the editor.

**Why:** Godot keeps a cache of script class names in the hidden `.godot/` folder, and only the editor rebuilds it. If an update adds new scripts and you press **Run** in the Project Manager, the cache is stale: the new scripts fail to load, and the game sits frozen on its first frame with a red-tinted screen and placeholder HUD values. Opening the editor once fixes it.
